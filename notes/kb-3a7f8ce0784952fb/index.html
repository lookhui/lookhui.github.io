# 核心要点

  • XPC Mach services are the single largest local 权限提升 attack surface on macOS. Most local root exploits in recent years went through vulnerable XPC services in LaunchDaemons. Every exposed method in a root daemon is a potential escalation vector.
  • The most common 漏洞 class in XPC services is insufficient client verification. The daemon should verify:
  • Audit 令牌 (not PID, which can be reused)
